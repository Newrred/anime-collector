3. Age information and minors

The date of birth entered during signup is used only to calculate age on your device and is not sent to MOEMOA’s servers or to Google. During signup, we temporarily process the country, declared age, assessment date, document versions and similar information. The account declaration record retains minimum items such as the age band and whether the minimum age was met. Self-declaration and acceptance records do not verify the identity of a user or guardian, their relationship or their authority to give consent.

Signup is currently available to users aged 14 or older in Korea and aged 13 or older in the United States and Thailand. Permitted countries and ages can be checked on the signup screen. The age at which signup is permitted may differ from the capacity to consent to a particular processing activity or enter a contract under local law. Where processing requires a parent’s or guardian’s consent by law, the relevant conditions apply, and minors’ legal rights are not restricted. Mentioning the Philippines, EU/EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland does not activate signup in those regions.

For processing necessary to perform a contract requested by a user in Thailand, we assess and apply the contractual ground under the relevant law. Separate processing that requires consent is subject to the Thai PDPA’s conditions for minors’ consent. A minimum age check is not used as verification of parental consent or blanket consent to all optional processing.

4. Device storage, cookies and sign-in data

Browser storage may retain local records, display settings and sign-in state. You can manage this through your browser’s data deletion and storage settings, but deleting the data may remove records stored only on the device or your sign-in state.

The encrypted signup cookie expires after 10 minutes, account linking data after 5 minutes, and encrypted sign-in handoff data after 2 minutes. Used server-side one-time data is deleted, and expired residual data is subject to hourly cleanup. A delayed or failed scheduled job may delay physical deletion, but expired data cannot be reused.

Connection information may be used to handle service requests. Our operating rule is not to include personal thoughts, original image content, private board names or search terms in ordinary application logs. If a support investigation requires detailed material, we limit it to the necessary scope and purpose.

Content you publish after checking the preview and scope can be viewed by visitors to public pages, including people who are not signed in. The items differ between quick sharing, boards and mini-homes, as explained in the Public Content Guide {{PUBLIC_POLICY_URL}}. Private records are not disclosed to other users, but this does not mean end-to-end encryption that prevents processing by the systems used to operate the service.

When you withdraw publication, new viewing is stopped for the corresponding public route. The operator cannot remotely retrieve copies that other people have already downloaded or copied. Do not publish another person’s real name, contact details, face or sensitive information without the necessary permission. When information is passed on to handle a report, we share only what is legally necessary and do not unnecessarily disclose the reporter’s personal data or intimate imagery.

We may otherwise provide information to the extent necessary where there is a separate ground, such as a legal requirement, a lawful request from an authority or protection of rights. Where consent is the ground for disclosure, we explain the recipient, purpose, information involved, period and consequences of refusal.

8. Your rights and how to make a request

Under applicable law, you may request access to or a copy of your information, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, review or objection, and portability or transfer where available. Please use the available account management features, the Rights Request Guide {{RIGHTS_URL}}, or godburgundy@gmail.com. You can also make a request by email if you cannot access your account.

The operator checks to the extent necessary whether a request comes from the person concerned or a properly authorized representative. Where a signed-in account or an existing contact channel is sufficient, we use that method first. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, tokens or unnecessary copies of identity documents. If further verification is necessary, we explain the reason, the minimum information required and a safe way to provide it.

Under Korean law, we provide access within 10 days of the request or explain the statutory grounds for postponement or refusal and how to challenge the decision. For correction, deletion and suspension of processing, we also communicate the result or the grounds for not carrying out the request and how to challenge it within 10 days, subject to the applicable statutory conditions. A lawful access or copy request under Thai law is handled without delay and, where no ground for refusal applies, within 30 days of receipt. Other regions and rights follow their applicable deadlines. These response deadlines are not the same as the physical deletion date for every recovery copy. Asking for information to identify data or carry out necessary verification does not arbitrarily pause or restart a statutory deadline.

Withdrawing consent does not retroactively make processing that was lawful before the withdrawal unlawful. If stopping the processing prevents us from providing the account service, we explain the effect. You may distinguish a request to close your account from a request to withdraw only a particular publication or optional processing activity. We do not treat every request as a request to delete the whole account.

If a justified reason such as legal retention or protection of another person’s rights prevents us from carrying out all or part of a request, we explain the reason, what can be done and how to appeal. You also have the right to seek remedies from competent bodies, including Korea’s Personal Information Protection Commission, personal data infringement reporting or dispute resolution bodies, and Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Committee. Please consult the relevant authority’s official guidance for detailed application routes.

9. Safeguards and personal data incidents

The service applies necessary safeguards such as access restrictions, encrypted communications, controls on operator privileges and private recovery copies, and application of deletion and withdrawal instructions. Private account data is distinguished from public content. This does not guarantee that no security incident or data loss will occur.

If we become aware of loss, theft, leakage or another personal data incident, we assess its scope and risks and take necessary containment, recovery and other measures. Where notification to users or supervisory authorities is required, we communicate the confirmed facts, effects, ways to reduce harm, measures taken and contact routes within the conditions and deadlines of applicable law, and supplement the notification with newly confirmed facts. We do not withhold all necessary initial notifications until every fact is confirmed.
